Ironwood Community Gym — front desk facts (fictional)

Membership plans
- Day pass: 12 dollars, valid until closing on the day of purchase. No classes included.
- Basic: 29 dollars per month. Gym floor and lockers. Classes cost 8 dollars each.
- Plus: 49 dollars per month. Gym floor, lockers and unlimited group classes.
- Student: 22 dollars per month with a valid student card. Same access as Basic.
- Joining fee: 15 dollars, waived for Plus memberships signed up in person.
- Freezing: Basic and Plus can be frozen for 1 to 3 months per year for 5 dollars per month while frozen. Day passes and Student plans cannot be frozen.
- Cancelling: 30 days notice, given at the front desk or by email to the membership office. No cancellation fee.

Opening hours
- Monday to Friday 5:30 to 22:00. Saturday 7:00 to 20:00. Sunday 8:00 to 18:00.
- The pool closes 30 minutes before the gym.

Weekly class schedule (all classes 45 minutes, studio B unless noted)
- Monday 7:00 Spin (spin room), 18:30 Strength Basics, 19:30 Yoga Flow
- Tuesday 12:15 Lunchtime HIIT, 18:00 Aqua Fit (pool)
- Wednesday 7:00 Spin (spin room), 18:30 Pilates, 19:30 Boxing Conditioning
- Thursday 12:15 Lunchtime HIIT, 18:00 Yoga Flow
- Friday 7:00 Spin (spin room), 17:30 Strength Basics
- Saturday 9:00 Family Swim (pool, under-12s with an adult), 10:00 Yoga Flow
- Sunday no classes
- Classes hold 16 people (spin: 12). Book up to 7 days ahead at the desk or in the member app. Cancel at least 2 hours before or it counts as a no-show; 3 no-shows in a month pause booking for 7 days.

Other
- Personal training: 45 dollars per session, 200 dollars for a pack of 5. Not included in any plan.
- Guests: Plus members may bring one guest per month for free; otherwise guests buy a day pass.
- Minimum age for the gym floor is 16. Under-16s may only attend Family Swim.
