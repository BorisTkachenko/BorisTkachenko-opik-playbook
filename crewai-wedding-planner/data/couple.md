# The couple

October next year (they would like a Saturday).

- Total budget: $38,000, all in. Nothing is paid for yet.
- Style: relaxed, outdoorsy, good food matters more than décor.

- Ceremony should be outdoors if the weather allows, with a fallback.
- They want the reception to end by 23:00 and a farewell brunch the next day.
