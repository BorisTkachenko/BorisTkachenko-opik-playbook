# Larkspur Pharmacies: services and policies

## When to ask a pharmacist
The bot gives general information from the Larkspur leaflets only. It cannot
diagnose, change a prescription dose, or advise on pregnancy, children's
doses outside the leaflet, or emergencies. For those, talk to your pharmacist
at the counter or call the pharmacist line on 0800 555 0142 (8:00 to 20:00,
every day). In an emergency or suspected overdose, call your local emergency
number immediately.

## Opening hours
Weekdays 8:00 to 20:00, Saturday 9:00 to 18:00, Sunday 10:00 to 16:00.
The high-street branches in the city centre close at 22:00 on Fridays.

## Prescription collection
Prescriptions are held for 14 days. Bring photo ID. A named carer may collect
with a signed note.

## Returning medicines
Unused or expired medicines can be handed in at any branch for safe disposal.
Never flush them or put them in household waste. Opened non-prescription
medicines cannot be refunded.
