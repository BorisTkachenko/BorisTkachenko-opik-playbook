# Brelovan 5 mg tablets (blood pressure)

Larkspur Pharmacies formulary code: LP-CV-005. Prescription only.

## Dosage notes
Take one tablet at the same time every day, with or without food. Never stop
suddenly or change the dose without your prescriber. If you miss a dose, take
it as soon as you remember unless it is almost time for the next one; never
double up. Dizziness when standing up is common in the first week; stand slowly.

## Interactions
Avoid grapefruit juice: it can make Brelovan stronger. Anti-inflammatory pain
relievers such as Corvadol can reduce its effect. Check with a pharmacist
before using decongestant cold remedies, which can raise blood pressure.

## Refills and storage
Larkspur offers automatic refills every 30 days; the pharmacist confirms them
by text message. Store at room temperature in the original container.
