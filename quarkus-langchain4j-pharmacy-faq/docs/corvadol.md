# Corvadol 400 mg (ibuprofen-type pain and fever tablets)

Larkspur Pharmacies formulary code: LP-AN-400. Over the counter, limit of two
boxes per customer per day.

## Dosage notes
Adults: one tablet every 6 to 8 hours with food, maximum 3 tablets (1,200 mg)
in 24 hours without medical advice. Not for children under 12.
Use the lowest dose for the shortest time. If pain lasts more than 5 days or
fever lasts more than 3 days, see a doctor.

## Interactions
Do not combine Corvadol with Brelovan or other blood pressure medicines
without asking a pharmacist: it can weaken their effect and strain the kidneys.
Do not take with Thinnex blood thinners, aspirin, or another anti-inflammatory.
Avoid in the last three months of pregnancy.

## Storage
Store at room temperature, below 30 C, away from direct sunlight.
Shelf life is printed on the carton; do not use after that month.
