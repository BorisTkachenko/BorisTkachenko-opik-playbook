# Thinnex 5 mg and Zolmetra 25 mg (prescription-only)

## Thinnex 5 mg tablets (blood thinner), code LP-CV-105
Take once or twice daily as prescribed, at the same times each day. Missed
dose: take it the same day, never double it. Carry a medicine alert card and
tell any dentist or doctor you take it. Report unusual bruising, nosebleeds
that do not stop, or blood in urine immediately. Avoid aspirin and Corvadol.

## Zolmetra 25 mg tablets (mood stabiliser), code LP-NE-025
Take in the evening with food. Do not stop suddenly. Avoid alcohol. Levels are
checked by blood test every 3 months; the pharmacist will remind you.
Store below 25 C, out of reach of children.
