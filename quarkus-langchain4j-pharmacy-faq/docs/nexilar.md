# Nexilar 20 mg capsules (stomach acid reducer)

Larkspur Pharmacies formulary code: LP-GI-020. Pharmacy-only: ask at the counter.

## Dosage notes
Adults: one capsule each morning, 30 minutes before breakfast, swallowed whole
with water. A course is 14 days. Do not repeat a course within 4 months
without seeing a doctor. Not recommended for children.
Signs that need a doctor straight away: black stools, vomiting blood,
difficulty swallowing, or unexplained weight loss.

## Interactions
Nexilar lowers stomach acid, which can reduce absorption of Ferrovita iron
tablets; take iron at least 4 hours apart. Tell the pharmacist if you take
Thinnex or Zolmetra, because Nexilar can change their levels.

## Storage
Keep the bottle tightly closed below 25 C. Use within 6 months of opening.
