# Veltrazine 10 mg tablets (non-drowsy antihistamine)

Larkspur Pharmacies formulary code: LP-AH-010. Over the counter.

## Dosage notes
Adults and children over 12: one 10 mg tablet once a day, with or without food.
Children 6 to 12: half a tablet once a day. Do not give to children under 6.
Do not take more than one tablet in 24 hours. People with kidney problems
should take one tablet every other day unless their pharmacist says otherwise.

## Interactions
Veltrazine can be taken with most pain relievers. Avoid combining it with
Dormiquel sleep aid or with alcohol, which can increase drowsiness even though
Veltrazine alone is non-drowsy for most people.

## Storage
Keep below 25 C in the original blister pack. A bathroom cabinet is too humid.
Discard 12 months after the box is opened.
