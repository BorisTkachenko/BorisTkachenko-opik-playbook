# Hop Schedules

## Bittering additions
Bittering hops go in at the start of a 60 minute boil. Use a high alpha acid variety such as Magnum at roughly 20 grams per 20 litre batch for a pale ale at 35 IBU. Longer boiling extracts more bitterness, so a 90 minute boil needs fewer grams for the same IBU.

## Flavour and aroma additions
Flavour hops are added with 15 minutes left in the boil and aroma hops at flameout, when the wort is below 80 C. Adding hops at flameout keeps volatile oils in the beer, so you get citrus and pine aroma with very little extra bitterness.

## Dry hopping
Dry hop on day 5 of fermentation, while the yeast is still active, using 4 grams per litre for a juicy IPA. Leave the hops in for 3 days only: longer contact adds a grassy, vegetal taste. Cold crash after removing them.
