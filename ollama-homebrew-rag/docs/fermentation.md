# Fermentation Temperatures

## Ale yeast
Ferment ale yeast at 18 to 20 C. Warmer than 23 C produces fusel alcohols and fruity esters that taste like nail polish remover. Measure the temperature of the beer itself with a stick-on thermometer, because fermentation runs 2 to 3 C warmer than the room.

## Lager yeast
Pitch lager yeast at 10 C and hold it between 9 and 12 C for two weeks. When gravity is within 4 points of the final gravity, raise the temperature to 18 C for a two day diacetyl rest, then lower it by 2 C per day to lagering temperature near 1 C for four weeks.

## When fermentation is finished
Fermentation is finished when the hydrometer reads the same value on three consecutive days, usually 1.010 to 1.014 for a pale ale. Never bottle on airlock activity alone, since a stalled airlock does not prove the gravity has stopped dropping.
