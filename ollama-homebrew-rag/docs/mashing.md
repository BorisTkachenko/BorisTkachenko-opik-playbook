# Mashing Guide

## Strike water and mash temperature
Heat strike water to about 74 C for a target mash temperature of 66 to 68 C. Stir for two minutes, check the temperature in three spots, and add a splash of cold or hot water to correct. A mash at 64 C gives a dry, crisp beer because beta amylase makes very fermentable sugars; a mash at 69 C leaves more dextrins and a fuller, sweeter body.

## Mash duration and the iodine test
Rest the mash for 60 minutes. To confirm conversion, put a drop of wort on a white plate and add a drop of iodine: if it stays amber the starch is converted, and if it turns blue-black give it another 10 minutes.

## Sparging and runoff
Heat sparge water to 77 C and rinse the grain bed slowly, aiming for a runoff of one litre every four minutes. Stop collecting when the runoff gravity falls below 1.008, because beyond that point you extract harsh tannins from the husks.
