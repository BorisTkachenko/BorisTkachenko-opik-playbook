[01:10] PRIYA: Each node reports its inlet temperature every minute. If the shelf drifts below twenty-six, a little script nudges a space heater on. Above thirty, it opens a vent. The starter is named Gerald, by the way.

[01:37] PRIYA: Gerald has survived two power cuts and one firmware update. The firmware update was worse.
[01:52] OSCAR: What's the loaf like? Does it taste like computers?
[01:56] PRIYA: It tastes like a slightly more sour country loaf. Customers say it has "character". I've stopped explaining.
[02:10] NELL: Okay, practical advice. Someone listening wants to do this. What do they need to know?
