# Visitor Information

**Placard 6.1 — Opening hours**

The Pellwick Lamp Museum is open Wednesday to Sunday, from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. The museum is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays, and on 25 and 26 December. The last entry is at 4 p.m.

**Placard 6.2 — Tickets**

Adult tickets cost 9 pounds. Children under 12 enter free when accompanied by an adult. The ticket also covers the climb to the lantern room at the top of the tower, which has 117 steps and no lift.

**Placard 6.3 — Access and dogs**

Galleries 1 to 5 are on the ground floor and are step-free. The lantern room is reachable by stairs only. Assistance dogs are welcome throughout the museum; other dogs may wait in the courtyard.
