# Gallery 5 — The Wreck of the Halcyon

**Placard 5.1 — The night of 9 November 1921**

The steam trawler Halcyon, out of Grimsby with a crew of eleven, struck the Widow's Teeth reef two kilometres south of Skerrow Point in the gale of 9 November 1921. The lamp was burning, but the storm threw so much spray that the beam was lost to the crew at close range.

**Placard 5.2 — The rescue**

The Pellwick lifeboat, the Mary Ansell, was launched at 3:40 a.m. and reached the wreck after two hours of rowing. Nine of the eleven crew were saved. Two men, the cook and a deckhand, were lost. A memorial plaque with their names hangs beside the doorway.

**Placard 5.3 — What was recovered**

The ship's bell, a brass compass and a tin lunch box were recovered from the reef at low tide in the spring of 1922. They are displayed together in the case below. The bell is engraved with the name Halcyon and the year 1903, when the trawler was built.
